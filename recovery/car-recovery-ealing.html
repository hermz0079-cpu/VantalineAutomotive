Ealing, Acton, Greenford, Southall and Northolt

We take recovery enquiries across Ealing and surrounding parts of West London, including Acton, Greenford, Southall and Northolt. The A40, A406 and approaches toward the M4 can all create very different recovery conditions from a simple residential pickup, so send an exact pin whenever possible.

If the car is in a retail park, underground parking, a narrow estate road or a busy roadside position, mention that before dispatch. We also need to know whether it rolls, steers, selects neutral and has any damaged or locked wheels.

Recovery options

  • Local breakdown recovery to a home address or repair garage.
  • Non-running cars requiring winch loading.
  • Accident-damaged vehicles where safe access and loading can be planned.
  • Vehicle pull-outs from soft ground or awkward positions.
  • Garage, dealer, bodyshop and private vehicle transport.
  • Jump starts and selected roadside assistance.

What does recovery cost?
