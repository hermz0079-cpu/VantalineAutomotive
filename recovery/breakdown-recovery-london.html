What to do when you break down

Move to a safe location if you can do so without creating additional risk. On high-speed roads, follow the instructions of the road operator or emergency services and do not remain in a dangerous live-traffic position just to wait for a recovery vehicle.

For the recovery quote, send a location pin or clear road/junction description. We also need to know whether the vehicle starts, rolls, steers and selects neutral. A car with a locked wheel or transmission stuck in park can require a different loading approach from a straightforward breakdown.

Where we recover from

We take enquiries across London, including North and West London, the A406, A40 and routes toward the M1, M4 and M25. Motorway and high-speed-road jobs are quoted separately because access, dead mileage and truck time can be materially different from an ordinary local pickup.

Typical breakdown movements

  • Vehicle to a chosen repair garage.
  • Vehicle back to a home address.
  • Non-runner collection after an electrical or mechanical fault.
  • Recovery after a failed jump start.
  • Recovery where a tyre/wheel issue prevents safe onward travel.
