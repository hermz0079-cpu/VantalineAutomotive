Planned movements for trade and private customers

Scheduled transport is different from an emergency breakdown. Because the date, route and collection details can usually be planned, we can price the whole movement around distance, truck positioning, vehicle condition and timing.

Typical transport jobs

  • Dealer or garage collection and delivery.
  • Auction or purchased-vehicle movements.
  • Non-runner transport where the rolling condition is known.
  • Bodyshop or repairer transfers.
  • Private sale collections and relocations.
  • Longer-distance London-to-UK or UK-to-London movements where commercially suitable.

What affects the transport quote?

We do not use a single blanket per-mile number for every route. A fair quote needs to consider the complete truck journey, including dead mileage, traffic, road charges, vehicle size and weight, loading difficulty, waiting and whether the job can be scheduled efficiently.

If the vehicle does not run, tell us whether it rolls, steers and selects neutral. Locked wheels, no keys, accident damage or low ground clearance should be disclosed before collection.
